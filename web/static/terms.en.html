These are the conditions for using fast question (https://fast-question.com). The service is run free of charge by yyj2046 as an individual (the "operator").

  1. 1

    The service

    A service for collecting and finding the questions and short answers that come up during work, in a repository per account. Your Claude Code and the web Claude panel read and write the same repository. There are no ads, and no fees are charged unless you separately agree to them.

  2. 2

    Sign-up and accounts

    Anyone aged 14 or older can sign up with a GitHub or Google account. At first each provider gets its own account; in the connection panel you can add a sign-in from another provider to your current account (the account the added sign-in used is deleted). Manage your sign-in accounts, personal access tokens and API keys yourself, and revoke them right away if you suspect a leak (tokens in the connection panel, API keys in the Claude panel). More than 5 sign-ups per hour from the same IP address are not accepted.

  3. 3

    Usage limits

    A question is one line of up to 300 characters, and an answer up to 800 characters and 6 lines (Hangul and CJK characters count as 2, other characters as 1). Each account can store up to 20,000 Q&As and 500 categories, and requests over a limit are refused. We announce any reduction of the limits in advance.

  4. 4

    Prohibited conduct

    Do not upload secrets such as passwords or API keys, other people's personal information, or content that infringes others' rights. Attempts to access other accounts, automated requests that disrupt operation, and account sharing are also prohibited. Content that Claude adds is refused when it looks like a secret, but not every case is caught.

    You own the rights to the content you store. The operator handles it only for storage, search, backup and failure handling, and does not publish it, sell it, use it for advertising or use it for AI training. You can export it from the connection panel at any time.

  9. 9

  10. 10

    Account deletion

If you ask by email within 30 days and your ownership of the account is confirmed, it can be restored; during that time you cannot sign up again with the same account. Export your data before deleting.
